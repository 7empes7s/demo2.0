01 · pushed further

Affichage

Every section is a separate notice pinned to the board. Black rules, hard shadows, yellow for what is yours, blue for what is next, red for the file number.

Pushed: the title is now set in uppercase display caps like a poster headline, the stage track is four physical blocks and the current one lifts, citation numbers are black tiles, and the stand buttons press down into the board when chosen. Dense content stays readable because every block has a hard edge and nothing floats.

  • Questions: a correct answer stamps a black tile; a miss shakes the option once.

03 · pushed further

Glass Chamber

Each section is a glass pane in a stack with real depth. The stage track is a lit rail where the current step glows. Move your pointer and the whole page tilts.

Pushed: a sticky frosted header, three slow light orbs that drift the whole length of the page, and "Next on the agenda" tinted warm so the eye goes there first. Glass handles density well because the blur separates panes from the background without borders. On a phone the tilt comes from the gyroscope.

04 · pushed further

Clay Commune

Moulded clay sections, a stage track made of four soft beads where the current one bounces, and three stance buttons in three clay colours that squash when pressed.

Pushed: the file page becomes a stack of soft objects you can almost pick up. Mint for what is settled, yellow for what is now, pink for what is at stake. Dense French legal text works here because the type is round and warm, and every section has generous radius and inner light.

07 · pushed further

Kinetic

Numbered sections in Syne caps, a ticker that never stops, striped progress on the current stage, and a huge date for the next meeting. Rows and answers invert to white on touch.

Pushed: the page reads like a broadcast rundown. The title rises in two lines, one solid and one outlined. Section headings carry their number in orange, so the long page is scannable at speed. Density is handled by rhythm: big, small, big, small.

08 · new · cousin of Affichage

Riso

Two-ink risograph print: fluorescent pink and deep blue on cream, slightly out of register, with halftone dots and paper grain. Anton for headlines, Space Mono for everything typed.

Where Affichage is a notice board, Riso is the zine someone printed at the youth centre. It has the same honesty and boldness but adds craft and a wink. Misregistration (the pink ghost behind the title) and dot screens are things no AI template does, so it is instantly human. Luxembourg's design scene loves this look.

09 · new · cousin of Glass Chamber

Holo

A trading-card holographic foil. The header is a 3D card that tilts and its rainbow sheen follows your finger. Everything else is matte black with gradient hairlines.

Glass is airy; Holo is precious. A file becomes a collectible: when a resident understands it, the card gets a foil finish. It is the most premium of the set, works beautifully on OLED phones, and gives the Arena a reason to exist beyond quizzing: collect the files that matter where you live.

10 · new · cousin of Kinetic

Broadcast

Live sports graphics for politics. A red LIVE bar, skewed lower-thirds, a scoreboard for the stage, condensed italic caps. The Chamber as a match you follow.

Kinetic is a festival; Broadcast is match day. It fits the loop you described: weekly matters, panels, decision week, published KPIs. Scores, standings and fixtures are the natural language for that. Everyone in Luxembourg already reads this visual grammar from RTL and football, so it needs no learning.

Verdict on the dense page

Of your four, Affichage and Glass Chamber carry the dense page best: hard edges and frosted panes both give twelve sections a clear order. Clay stays charming but needs the sections trimmed. Kinetic is the hardest to keep legible when there is this much text; it wants a reduced mode for long files.

If I had to ship one tomorrow: Affichage, with Riso's pink misregistration on the headers as the signature detail. Cheapest to build, boldest on a phone, and unmistakably not a template.

If you want the wow: Glass Chamber for the structure with Holo's foil card as the reward for understood files.

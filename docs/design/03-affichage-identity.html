Affichage,made ours

You have seen neo-brutalism before: white boxes, black offset shadows, a primary yellow. That is a template. Ours is not boxes, it is paper. Every element is a sheet pasted on a public wall: condensed poster headlines, a second coloured sheet behind instead of a shadow, staples and tacks, torn edges, and motion that pastes, presses, peels and tears. Below: the mark, the material, the type, the motion system, and both screens rebuilt in it.

Torn sheet

Header sheets tear along the bottom. The tear is a clip path, so it costs nothing and never repeats the same way twice.

Stapled sheet

A second sheet behind, turned under one degree. Navy for official things, amber for yours. Two staples hold it.

Red backing

Used once per screen at most: the thing with a date on it. Red is loud, so it is rationed.

04

Motion: six verbs

Every animation in the app is one of these six. Each has a fixed duration and easing, so the whole product moves like one material. Tap replay to see each.

Paste

Week 41Pasted on, settles with a slight overshoot and a roller sweep.

When: anything new appears: a screen, a list entry, the Companion's answer. 700 ms, overshoot easing.

Press

When: you choose: a stance, an answer, a topic. The sheet goes flat onto its backing and a tack lands. 120 ms, linear.

Peel

Week 42The next sheet was already underneath.

When: moving between screens or weeks. The old sheet peels, the new one was always under it. 800 ms.

Tear

06

How it lands in the app

Behind a flag, so the current look stays live until you switch. Three small PRs, each checked on a phone at 375 px.

1

Tokens and type

New colour tokens (paper, ink, amber, navy, red), the three faces, the paper grain, light and dark. Nothing else changes yet, so this PR is safe and reviewable in minutes.

2

Sheets and marks

The pasted sheet replaces the card. Staple, tack, torn edge and the wordmark as small components. "This week" and the file page adopt them.

3

Motion

The six verbs as CSS keyframes with one shared timing file, applied to appear, choose, navigate, dismiss, count and wait. Reduced-motion turns them all off.
